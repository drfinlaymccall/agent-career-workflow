# Worked application example

**Entirely synthetic.** This case demonstrates reasoning and document separation, not a real candidate or vacancy.
The CLI demonstration is also synthetic and may use a different set of invented records.

## Situation

Example Candidate coordinated volunteers at Fictional Harbour Services.
They want an operations coordinator role at Fictional Orchard Support.
They prefer a practical coordination role over managing a large department.
Work rights, salary expectations and other sensitive practical facts remain private and must be confirmed separately.

The invented vacancy asks for volunteer scheduling, process documentation and experience with a named CRM.
Its example URL is `https://careers.orchard.example/operations-001`.
The example URL is not a working source. No availability or closing-date claim is made.

## Requirement-to-evidence matrix

| Requirement | Strongest supported example | Evidence status | Claim boundary | Drafting decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coordinate schedules | Reconciled weekly volunteer availability and shift requirements | Synthetic document-verified scenario | No evidence of multi-site workforce management | Lead with the coordination task |
| Document a repeatable process | Wrote a handover checklist and revised it after coordinator feedback | Synthetic human-confirmed scenario | No measured efficiency improvement supplied | Describe the checklist and revision, not a numerical outcome |
| Use the employer's named CRM | Used a spreadsheet-based contact register | Transferable only | Does not establish experience in the named CRM | Keep the gap explicit in private review; do not list CRM proficiency |

The strongest unused evidence is organising a one-off community meeting.
It supports coordination but is less directly relevant than recurring scheduling.
Omission from this application does not remove it from the Master CV.

## Applicant-facing CV excerpt

### Relevant experience

**Volunteer Coordinator, Fictional Harbour Services**

- Reconciled weekly volunteer availability with shift requirements and communicated schedule changes.
- Wrote a handover checklist and revised it after feedback from another coordinator.
- Maintained a spreadsheet-based contact register for authorised internal use.

Dates are deliberately omitted from this teaching example. A real CV must use confirmed dates.

## Applicant-facing letter excerpt

I am interested in the Operations Coordinator role because it combines practical scheduling with clear service processes.
At Fictional Harbour Services, I reconciled volunteer availability with weekly shift requirements and communicated changes.
I also wrote a handover checklist, then revised it in response to coordinator feedback.
These examples show how I organise recurring work and make operational guidance easier for colleagues to use.

This excerpt makes a supported argument. It does not claim expertise in the employer's CRM or invent performance metrics.
A real letter needs the candidate's own motivation, verified context and the employer's actual instructions.

## Private review

- Confirm whether named CRM experience is an essential screening requirement.
- Ask the candidate whether any direct CRM evidence has been missed.
- If appropriate, propose a small authorised practice task using dummy data.
- Record new practice as recent learning, not historical professional delivery.
- Respect employer restrictions on AI-assisted preparation.
- Review the complete CV and letter together before export.
- Inspect generated DOCX/PDF text and every rendered page before release.

## State and approval

The synthetic candidate's expression of interest may justify preparation after verification and fit review.
It does not establish proficiency, authorise an upload or constitute an application.
After an actual submission, record the candidate's report and exact materials submitted.
An employer receipt and any later outcome require their own evidence.
