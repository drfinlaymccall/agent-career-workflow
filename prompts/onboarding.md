# Onboarding interview

## Bounded permissions and output contract

Work only with the candidate, paths and sources explicitly authorised for this session. Default to an interview and proposed Markdown content: do not initialise a vault, read unrelated files, connect an account, upload personal material, search the web or schedule/send anything without permission for that operation. Never apply, contact an employer or participate in an assessment. Do not request secrets in chat.

Return a staged, candidate-correctable onboarding record: confirmed context, unresolved questions, proposed factual sources, role lanes, access/source decisions and the next approval gate. Claim a saved file only after it exists and has been checked. If approval or a necessary fact is missing, stop that action and ask one focused question; continue only unrelated permitted work.

## Inputs to resolve

- Intended private vault location, outside the public clone, or “not yet chosen”.
- Existing profile/CV documents the candidate permits you to read, or “interview only”.
- Host model/tool data handling and any local-only or redaction requirement.
- Current goal: initial career exploration, active search, a particular application or a review of direction.
- Allowed operations for this session: read, proposed edits, approved local writes, public retrieval. Treat unspecified permissions as absent.

Use [the onboarding guide](../docs/Methodology.md), [evidence method](../docs/Private%20vault.md) and [integration decisions](../docs/Integrations.md). The public repository's examples are synthetic, not candidate facts.

## Work in stages

1. **Agree privacy and scope.** Explain that local files may still be transmitted to a hosted model. Ask what may be read, stored and used in a search or digest. Offer Markdown-only operation. Obtain permission for a private location before using `init`; check the installed CLI's help rather than inventing options.
2. **Understand practical context.** Ask a few questions at a time about location/commute, hours, contract pattern, start date, compensation constraints if the candidate wishes to state them, accessibility/scheduling needs and exclusions. Ask for the practical condition, not an unnecessary sensitive explanation. Mark each as firm, preferred, exploratory or unknown.
3. **Understand professional direction.** Ask what work the candidate has actually done, wants more/less of, finds meaningful and can demonstrate. Separate responsibility, contribution, training, tool exposure and delivered outcomes. Ask for authentic motivation and voice rather than inventing them.
4. **Establish facts.** With permission, inspect the current factual CV and a small candidate-selected evidence set. Propose `profile/Master CV.md` and individual `evidence/` records with claim, source, status and boundary. An old letter is a lead to evidence, not proof. Ask about consequential conflicts; do not repair unknown dates or inflate claims.
5. **Explore lanes.** Compare a small set of core, adjacent and optional exploratory lanes against constraints, direct evidence and gaps. Ask which the candidate genuinely wants. Do not treat a stretch as permission to relax eligibility or make paid training recommendations without a verified need.
6. **Choose sources and integrations.** Agree a bounded diversified source contract, official-verification gate and separate employer watchlist. Explain that public connectors produce leads, broad search uses host tools, Obsidian is optional, SQLite is local/rebuildable, and Gbrain/vector/cloud search are not implemented. Account linkage, scheduling and delivery each need separate informed consent.
7. **Rehearse only after approval.** Produce a no-send local trial within the agreed scope, validate records and inspect output. Keep a genuinely empty shortlist empty. If using a demo to explain controls, use a separate synthetic vault. Dashboard feedback copying is not a saved event.

At each stage, summarise what the candidate said, label assumptions and ask for corrections before treating the summary as approved. Reuse existing approved information rather than re-interviewing needlessly.

## Deliverables and completion gate

Propose or, when authorised, save the five profile documents: `Personal context.md`, `Professional context.md`, `Master CV.md`, `Role preferences.md` and `Access decisions.md`. Also identify evidence records, the source-contract decision note, unresolved facts and a separate employer watchlist where relevant.

End with:

- **Approved:** context, factual boundaries, lanes and data operations actually approved.
- **Created or changed:** exact private paths and verification results, or clearly labelled proposed content.
- **Not enabled:** account access, cloud search, scheduling and delivery unless separately verified.
- **Needs the candidate:** only the next meaningful decisions/questions.

Onboarding is complete only after approval of scope, context, factual foundation, lanes and sources, plus an inspected no-send rehearsal. Optional integrations may remain off. Do not promise employment, interviews or ATS success.
